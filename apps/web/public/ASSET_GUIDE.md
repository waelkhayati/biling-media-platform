# Frontend assets

Replace the marked placeholders in the homepage with approved Biling-owned assets. Do not treat
placeholder artwork as official production photography or brand artwork. The supplied
`/public/logo.png` is used as provided; do not redraw or alter it.

- `brand/` — official logo files; do not redraw or approximate the logo.
- `images/shows/` — approved show photography and key art.
- `images/episodes/` — approved episode thumbnails.
- `images/hosts/` — approved portraits and contributor photography.
- `textures/` — licensed or Biling-created print/grain textures.

Use descriptive filenames and confirm usage rights before adding production media.
